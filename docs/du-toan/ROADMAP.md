# Lo trinh B1-B6

## Nguyen tac lap lich

Thoi luong duoi day la uoc tinh cong suc, khong phai cam ket lich. Cac giai doan phai chay noi tiep theo gate; khong lam song song nhung task cung tac dong vao workbook hoac mo hinh du lieu.

| Giai doan | Muc tieu | Task | Uoc tinh | Dieu kien qua gate |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Khoi dong | Co ho so dieu phoi va baseline | DT-000, DT-101 | 1-2 ngay | Workbook chuan, checkpoint va ket qua ky vong da duoc ghi |
| B1 | Nhan dien workbook va ho so du an ben vung | DT-102 den DT-105 | 4-6 ngay | Doi/xoa/them sheet van nhan dung vai tro; save/reopen khong mat thong tin |
| B2 | Engine goi quy dinh co phien ban | DT-201 den DT-205 | 6-9 ngay | Chon dung goi theo ngay, pin ho so cu, khong nang cap ngam |
| B3 | Du lieu BQP 2021 va 2025 | DT-301 den DT-305 | 10-15 ngay | Du lieu doi chieu hai nguon, co truy vet va bao cao sai khac |
| B4 | Luong lap du toan hoan chinh | DT-401 den DT-407 | 12-18 ngay | VL-NC-M -> DG Can/Nuoc -> Gia DT TC -> THKP-TC khop workbook chuan |
| B5 | Ghi Excel, kiem soat va audit | DT-501 den DT-505 | 7-10 ngay | Ghi batch, khong loi COM, co truy vet va so sanh chuyen doi |
| B6 | Cap nhat va thuong mai hoa | DT-601 den DT-606 | 8-12 ngay | Goi cap nhat ky so, test tuong thich, bo cai va goi ho tro dat nghiem thu |

Tong uoc tinh: 48-72 ngay cong. B3 la phan ton nhieu thoi gian nhat vi du lieu phap ly phai duoc nhap va kiem tra cheo, khong chi sao chep bang.

## Gate bat buoc

### Gate B1

- Moi vai tro sheet co ID ngu nghia doc lap voi ten sheet.
- File chua danh dau co wizard anh xa.
- Doi ten sheet van nhan ra; xoa sheet phai yeu cau anh xa lai.
- Workbook luu va mo lai van giu project profile.

### Gate B2

- Goi quy dinh la bat bien va co `EffectiveFrom`, `EffectiveTo`, checksum, nguon.
- Ho so cu pin dung goi da ap dung.
- Co resolver ngay hieu luc va xu ly chuyen tiep.
- Chuyen goi phai tao ban sao hoac checkpoint va cho xem diff.

### Gate B3

- Co goi 2021 va goi hop nhat hieu luc 28/10/2025.
- Moi record co van ban, phu luc, bang/muc va dong nguon.
- Dinh muc, chi phi, may, dia ban va quy trinh duoc tach module.
- Kiem tra cheo doc lap dat, khong con muc chua xac minh.

### Gate B4

- Tra cuu ma va tinh chi phi khong phu thuoc sheet `Tracuu`, `ChiPhi` lam nguon chinh.
- Luong tinh tu gia dau vao den tong hop kinh phi chay tron ven.
- Hai che do can/nuoc va cong tac lay he so co can cu ro rang.
- Ket qua khop baseline da chot trong sai so cho phep.

### Gate B5

- Excel state duoc khoi phuc ke ca khi co exception.
- Ghi du lieu theo batch va khong tao cong thuc `#REF!` moi.
- Moi ket qua co the truy nguoc ve goi phap ly va profile gia.
- Migration co preview, log, rollback/checkpoint.

### Gate B6

- Goi cap nhat offline duoc xac thuc chu ky va checksum.
- Phien ban cu va moi cai song song; rollback duoc.
- Dat ma tran Windows/Office/locale bat buoc.
- Bo cai, license, support package va tai lieu nguoi dung san sang phat hanh.
